🧪 Build Your Data Foundation
Self-reported quizzes reveal patterns — but only objective data catches anomalies early. Pair this baseline with regular blood work and continuous wearable tracking to spot issues months or years before symptoms appear.
🩸
Get Comprehensive Blood Work
Ask your doctor (or a direct-to-consumer lab service) for these markers at least once a year:
  • Lipid panel: total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides
  • Metabolic: fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin, ALT
  • Hormones: thyroid (TSH, free T3/T4), testosterone (men), estradiol (women)
  • Nutrients: vitamin D, B12, folate, ferritin, magnesium (RBC)
  • Inflammation: hs-CRP, homocysteine
  • Foundation: full blood count, kidney (eGFR, creatinine), liver (AST, ALT, GGT)
⌚
Track Continuously With a Wearable
Wearables catch trends no single test can. Wear one for 30+ days to establish your true baseline:
  • Sleep architecture — deep & REM minutes, not just total hours
  • HRV (heart rate variability) — your daily recovery & stress score
  • Resting heart rate — rising 5+ bpm signals poor recovery or illness
  • Respiratory rate — spikes flag infection early
  • SpO2 (blood oxygen) — overnight dips reveal sleep apnea risk
  • Cardio load / VO₂max trend — true aerobic fitness over time
Devices: Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit — pick one you'll actually wear.
